# SVORA brand — what may be said and how it looks

Facts: `settings/brand.yaml` is the only source (prices, hours, address, team, languages, links).
Anything not there is not a fact — no awards, years of experience, product brands, guarantees,
"best in Warsaw", discount codes. A promotion seen on Instagram is not a fact until the owner confirms
it is bookable on Fresha.

## Positioning in one line

**Nude & Crazy** — the studio where the clean nude/French set is flawless *and* the extra-long, 3D,
sculpted set exists at all. Premium, warm, direct. Ukrainian founder and team; speaks UA / RU / PL / EN;
Wola, open daily 9–21; online booking on Fresha in a minute.

## Voice

- Client-first-person social proof works best for this niche ("мене всі питають де роблю нігті",
  "koleżanka poleciła"); then curiosity ("як вони це роблять?"); then a plain booking ask.
- Short sentences. Concrete nouns (hybrid 190 zł, Wola, today). No superlatives, no exclamation stacks.
- PL informal "Ty". UA default Cyrillic, warm "ти". RU neutral, separate ad set. EN British, for expats.
- Describe the studio, never the reader: "українська команда" yes; "ти українка?" no (Meta policy too).

## Visual

- Colors: Vivid Orange `#FF8030` (CTA, one accent word), Peach `#FFBE98` (secondary text), Cream
  `#F7F1EB` (text on dark), Black `#1A1A1A` (shade/background). Nothing else.
- Fonts: Roboto Condensed (all languages, weights 500–800); Antonio Bold for Latin display lines only
  (no Cyrillic); Smile Power is decorative ASCII-only — avoid in ads.
- Logo: `brand/logos/light.png` on photos/video, `orange.png` on cream, `black.png` on peach. Never
  regenerate the logo; overlay it.
- The nails are the hero: real SVORA work, unaltered design, hand in motion. Text ≤ 6 words on screen,
  inside the Reels safe zone, one orange pill CTA.
- Hashtags, emoji on the image, before/after implying treatment, other salons' work: never.
