## Video quality bar (what "not very great" turns into)

1. **Motion with intent**, not a slow drift: one of — push-in that ends on a macro, a hand turn that catches a light
   sweep, a two-shot (4 s macro → 4 s wide, two Seedance generations stitched), or a real reel cut on the beat.
2. **Hook by 0.5 s**: the sticker is on screen from 0.3 s with a 200 ms fade; the clip starts in motion, never from a frozen frame.
3. **Sound on every video**: a cleared bed from `brand/music/` under every clip (ducked under speech), −14 LUFS. Silent videos do not pass review.
4. **End card** for price/pick/before-after: the last 1.5 s is a `static-hero` frame with the CTA, so the CTA is readable even when the overlay was missed.
5. **Nails never covered**: `describe-image.mjs` sets `place`; the reviewer checks it. Since the 2026-10-09 review the
   hook and the CTA are two separate small blocks (hook at `place`, CTA alone at the bottom of the safe zone) — a single
   tall block was rejected across the board ("text overlays hands too much").
6. **Seedance prompts** per clip: camera + hand + light, "design identical", 8 s, 720×1280, 2 candidates when the first is soft; the manifest keeps the losing attempt's cost.
7. Captions on talking heads aligned to speech within 0.3 s (nudge the transcript segments by hand when Gemini drifts).

## The loop

```
wave N  ──►  node scripts/review.mjs build          # creatives/_work/review/index.html — every draft, playable, with 👍 👎 + note
        ──►  you: open the page, mark, press "Export", paste the JSON into the chat (or save scratch/review-N.json)
        ──►  node scripts/review.mjs apply <json>   # approved / rejected + notes into the manifests
        ──►  I read every rejection note, fix or regenerate, localise the approved masters → wave N+1
```

Rules: a rejected master is fixed once; if it fails twice its slot goes to another angle. A master is localised to all
its languages only after its first language is approved (translations are cheap; rejections are not).
