## Pipeline learnings from wave-1 tests (2026-10-09)

- Nano Banana (`gemini-3.1-flash-image`) extends a 3:4 original to 9:16 faithfully for $0.07; output is 768×1376.
- Seedance 2.5, 8 s, 720×1280: $1.85 per clip (output 716×1284; finish crops to 720×1280). Nails and designs stay identical; motion is subtle and premium.
- Provider filters: `generate_audio: true` can fail with "output audio may be related to copyright" (2 of 4 jobs) → retry without audio; the word **"nude" in a prompt is rejected** as sensitive input; one lap-framed photo was refused as "sensitive video" twice → pick another source rather than retry. Failed jobs were not charged.
- Instagram JPEGs are UltraHDR (gain map): ImageMagick cannot read them directly — use ffmpeg for previews; Playwright/Chrome and the APIs read them fine.
