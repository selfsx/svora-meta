# SVORA NAILS: Warsaw competitor and channel research

Date: 2026-10-08. Scope: nail studios competing for the same clients as SVORA NAILS (Jana Kazimierza 25B/U3, Wola; Fresha; hybrid manicure 190 PLN; "Nude & Crazy"; PL/EN/UA site, staff speak PL/UA/RU/EN; open daily 9-21, per [svora.place](https://svora.place)).

Method: ~45 web searches in Polish, Ukrainian, Russian and English plus direct fetches of Booksy/Fresha listing pages, Instagram search snippets and industry sources. Every claim about a specific business carries a URL. Instagram follower counts come from search-index snippets and can lag the live profile. Booksy/Fresha prices are snapshots from listing pages.

Caveat on the Meta Ad Library: both fetches (`q=manicure warszawa` and `q=paznokcie warszawa`, country=PL) returned HTTP 403, so section 3 uses secondary evidence (platform mechanics, Instagram bio offers, Booksy listing promotions, agency job briefs) and says so.

Reference points (all Warsaw, hybrid manicure, single colour unless stated):

- "Nail Spa" and "Hola Nails": no Warsaw chain with those exact names surfaced. The "Nail Spa" results are independent salons (Nail Spa Mokotów on Fresha; Marzena Kanclerska Nail Spa at 160 PLN, [Booksy](https://booksy.com/pl-pl/275788_marzena-kanclerska-nail-spa_paznokcie_3_warszawa)). "Hola Nails" returned nothing in Poland.

**Mid-market (130-170 PLN)**
- kb.pl aggregator estimate for Warsaw: 156 PLN net / 169 PLN gross (published 29.04.2026, "estimates based on offers found online") ([kb.pl Warsaw](https://kb.pl/cenniki/miejskie/paznokcie/warszawa/)). National range 119.67-182.89, average 141.67 ([kb.pl national](https://kb.pl/cenniki/manicure-hybrydowy/)).
- Wola/Bemowo listings: Oui Oui 140, Kolory Zachwytu 145, Lash In Flash 150, MORETTI 155, Pala.nails 160, REFORM 160 (standard), Anna Nail Studio 160→140.80, Pompi's Place 160+ ([Booksy Wola](https://booksy.com/pl-pl/s/paznokcie/32248_wola), [Booksy Odolany](https://booksy.com/pl-pl/s/paznokcie/30692_odolany)).
- Fresha: Tina Ng 160, Yaroslav 160, whynot. 149-159, Senchilova 170, M&D from 170 ([Fresha Warsaw nails](https://www.fresha.com/lp/en/bt/nails/in/pl-warsaw/ko%C5%82o)).

**Premium (170-215 PLN)**
- Klinika Paznokci 170 (short nails only); Nail Space Studio 170 ([Booksy](https://booksy.com/pl-pl/183479_nail-space-studio_paznokcie_3_warszawa)); PRO BEAUTY 179 incl. removal; REFORM TOP 180; SAMALEO 180 (hybrid + French); MANIFEST from 180; The Kitchen 190 stylist / 215 top stylist / 225-250 with IBX.

**Where SVORA sits:** 190 PLN is the top of the Wola market and equal to The Kitchen's base tier in Śródmieście. Only The Kitchen's "top stylist" tier (215) and add-on variants exceed it. The two nearest neighbours on Jana Kazimierza are 179 (PRO BEAUTY, removal included) and 129 for a classic manicure (Arcobaleno). SVORA charges removal separately (60 PLN, [svora.place](https://svora.place)), so an all-in returning-client hybrid is 250 PLN vs 179 at PRO BEAUTY, a gap that competitors' listings make visible. SVORA's CLEAN manicure at 120 is in line with The Kitchen's classic (110-140). Extensions from 300 and "Extra Long & Crazy Shape" 380 have no direct comparable in Wola listings; Pala's gel build-up tops out at 210 and ASALODA's extensions start at 250 ([Fresha](https://www.fresha.com/lp/en/bt/nails/in/pl-warsaw/ko%C5%82o)), so SVORA is clearly premium on length/shape work.

Implication: 190 is defensible only if the ad creative sells what the 140-160 studios do not (art, shape, length, bilingual service, guarantee). It is not defensible on "hybrid manicure" as a commodity keyword.

## 3. How competitors market on Meta/Instagram

**Ad Library status:** The Meta Ad Library pages for `manicure warszawa` and `paznokcie warszawa` (country PL, active ads) returned HTTP 403 to automated fetches, so active-ad creative could not be inspected directly. A manual check in a logged-in browser is recommended; the Polish-language guides confirm the library shows platforms, run dates, advertiser and creative, but not spend or targeting for non-political ads ([shopify.com/pl blog](https://www.shopify.com/pl/blog/biblioteka-reklam-meta), [blabla.ai](https://blabla.ai/pl/blog/meta-ad-library)).

**What the secondary evidence shows:**

- Offers in bios and listings cluster around a 10% first-visit discount or a cheap add-on: Beauty2go Warszawa Centrum "-10% na pierwszą wizytę" plus a 250 PLN mani+pedi anchor ([IG](https://www.instagram.com/beauty2gowarsaw/)); Rox Beauty Bar "hand SPA for 10 zł for new clients" ([IG](https://www.instagram.com/rox.beauty.bar/)); Pala.nails -10% if you book by phone or Instagram rather than Booksy ([Booksy](https://booksy.com/pl-pl/219863_pala-nails-yuliia_paznokcie_3_warszawa)); Atianos Nails first-visit hybrid 130 instead of 160 ([Booksy](https://booksy.com/pl-pl/295958_atianos-nails_paznokcie_3_warszawa)).
- Booksy listing promotions are standardised as "Zaoszczędź nawet X%" (5-15%) shown as strike-through prices, plus gift cards, cashback and Klarna badges; the top 20 Wola results are all sponsored placements ([Booksy Wola](https://booksy.com/pl-pl/s/paznokcie/32248_wola)).
- Landing destinations: Booksy is the default for Polish-run salons; Instagram DM is the default for diaspora accounts (@warszawa.nails: "для записи писать в директ", [IG](https://www.instagram.com/warszawa.nails/)); Fresha is used by a minority (Larte, M&D, whynot., SVORA, Senchilova) ([Fresha](https://www.fresha.com/lp/en/bt/nails/in/pl-warsaw/ko%C5%82o)).
- Agency briefs posted by Polish beauty salons on Useme describe the typical set-up: Meta Lead Ads and campaigns whose goal is "pozyskiwanie nowych klientek" with bookings routed to Booksy ([Useme brief 1](https://useme.com/pl/jobs/specjalista-meta-ads-beauty-local-naprawa-i-prowadzenie-kampanii-lead-ads,145553/), [Useme brief 2](https://useme.com/pl/jobs/kampania-meta-ads-dla-lokalnego-salonu-beauty-szkolenie-z-prowadzenia-reklam,125669/)); a non-beauty local case on the same platform ran on 763 PLN budget with 4.83% CTR, indicating the budget scale small local businesses use ([Useme](https://useme.com/en/roles/contractor/julia-marciniak,308921/)).
- Booksy's own guidance for beauty Meta ads (2019, dated but still the reference Polish salons use): start with reach/awareness for a new location, use a promotional offer to drive first visits, carousel for multiple services, video for interiors, exclude existing clients when prospecting, link to a price list or booking page with UTM ([biz.booksy.com](https://biz.booksy.com/pl-pl/blog/skuteczne-kampanie-na-facebooku-w-branzy-beauty)).
- Platform economics shape the hooks: Booksy Boost is reported at 45% net of a new client's first visit ([meetergo, a competitor source](https://meetergo.com/pl/blog/booksy-cennik)); Fresha charges a one-time new-client fee on the first completed appointment for marketplace-sourced clients, with third parties quoting ~20% ([Fresha help center](https://www.fresha.com/help-center/knowledge-base/billing-and-fees/101357-marketplace-new-client-fees), [Medium](https://medium.com/@asbaines/the-true-cost-of-fresha-276d6856bcc0)). This is why Pala rewards phone/IG bookings: a 10% discount is cheaper than the marketplace fee.
- Content style on the diaspora accounts is service-list bios in Cyrillic with flags (🇺🇦🇵🇱), sterilisation and experience claims, and DM CTAs ([@balyk_nails](https://www.instagram.com/balyk_nails/), [@warszawa.nails](https://www.instagram.com/warszawa.nails/)). Polish premium accounts (The Kitchen, REFORM) lean on concept naming and tiered stylists rather than discounts.

## 4. How Ukrainian/Russian-speaking Warsaw finds salons

**Scale of the Ukrainian beauty supply side:** 12.1K CEIDG business registrations by Ukrainian citizens under beauty PKD codes between 24.02.2022 and 30.09.2025, 11.5% of all Ukrainian registrations; 81.7K active Ukrainian-run firms in Poland ([Wiadomości Kosmetyczne, 03.10.2025](https://www.wiadomoscikosmetyczne.pl/biznes-kosmetyki/ministerstwo-rozwoju-i-technologii-rosnie-liczba-zakladanych-przez-obywateli-i-obywatelki-ukrainy-firm-w-branzy-kosmetycznej-2529160)). Competition for the diaspora client is therefore mostly from other Ukrainian masters, not from Polish chains.

**Channels:**
- Instagram with DM booking is the primary discovery channel for diaspora clients; the largest account found (@warszawa.nails, ~40K) has no booking platform at all ([IG](https://www.instagram.com/warszawa.nails/)). Other UA/RU-bio accounts in the 2-7K range: @liuda_zinchuk_ (~7.3K), @corner.warsaw (~7.7K), @nail.natalia (~7.2K), @pinkberry_pl (~7.2K) ([IG search](https://www.instagram.com/liuda_zinchuk_/), [IG](https://www.instagram.com/corner.warsaw/), [IG](https://www.instagram.com/nail.natalia/)).
- Facebook: pages "Українці в Польщі Ukraińcy w Polsce" (~40K followers), "Ukrainian in Poland" (~35K, Warsaw-based), "Українці в Варшаві" (~2.7K) ([FB](https://www.facebook.com/Ukraincywpolsce/), [FB](https://www.facebook.com/ukrainianinpolandpl/), [FB](https://www.facebook.com/p/%D0%A3%D0%BA%D1%80%D0%B0%D1%97%D0%BD%D1%86%D1%96-%D0%B2-%D0%92%D0%B0%D1%80%D1%88%D0%B0%D0%B2%D1%96-100064069933452/)). Closed groups do not index, so member counts for recommendation groups could not be verified.
- Telegram: the "Наші в світі" network claims 500K+ subscribers across 17 country channels including Poland, sells ads to Ukrainian-run businesses abroad, and runs chats where members share recommendations ([UNIAN, 09.01.2024, promotional piece](https://www.unian.ua/society/informaciyniy-vakuum-ukrajinciv-za-kordonom-novini-ukrajini-amp-12505431.html)).
- Booksy vs Fresha: Booksy is the most-used scheduling tool in Poland ([wmtips](https://www.wmtips.com/technologies/compare/booksy-vs-fresha)); Fresha reported a 296% YoY rise in active Polish partners and opened a larger Warsaw office in July 2026 ([01net](https://www.01net.it/fresha-accelerates-european-expansion-with-new-warsaw-office-and-appointment-of-maciej-walczewski-as-general-manager-for-eastern-europe/)). Several Ukrainian solo masters use Fresha (Senchilova, Yaroslav, UA Beauty by Khrystyna, [Fresha](https://www.fresha.com/lp/en/bt/nails/in/pl-warsaw/ko%C5%82o)), but the high-review Wola studios are on Booksy.
- Review habits: Ukrainian/Russian-language reviews appear on Booksy pages of Ukrainian masters (Pala.nails, [Booksy](https://booksy.com/pl-pl/219863_pala-nails-yuliia_paznokcie_3_warszawa)), so clients do review on Polish platforms when prompted.

**Language evidence (Ukrainian vs Russian):**
- EWL/University of Warsaw 2022: 90% of refugees can communicate in Russian, 55% in English, 9% declared good Polish; 68% of employed refugees can use Ukrainian at work ([Polskie Radio](https://www.polskieradio.pl/395/7789/Artykul/3039173), [WBJ](https://wbj.pl/more-than-23-of-ukrainian-refugees-speak-their-native-language-at-work/post/135787)).
- Ukraine-wide Rating polls: Russian-only at home fell from 26% (end-2021) to 18% (2022) to 12% (2024) ([Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/poll-ukrainian-russian-12), [Interfax](https://en.interfax.com.ua/news/general/364974.html)); two-thirds of bilinguals plan to switch fully to Ukrainian ([Espreso](https://global.espreso.tv/two-thirds-of-bilingual-ukrainians-plan-to-switch-exclusively-to-ukrainian-poll)).
- Levchuk (PAN, 2023) documents the expanded status of Ukrainian in Polish public space after 2022, including advertising ([CEEOL](https://www.ceeol.com/search/article-detail?id=1208282)); his 2021 paper already recorded Ukrainian-language ads from banks, telecoms and cinemas because most migrants had only basic Polish ([CEEOL](https://www.ceeol.com/search/article-detail?id=1021646)).
- No 2025-2026 survey of language preference in advertising among Ukrainians in Poland was found. The practical reading of the evidence above: Ukrainian is the safe lead language for diaspora creatives; Russian remains widely understood (and is the home language of most Belarusian clients) but Russian-first ad copy carries reputational risk with the Ukrainian majority. Competitors handle this by listing languages ("PL/UA/RU/EN") rather than writing Russian copy, and by using Ukrainian plus Polish in captions ([@pinkberry_pl](https://www.instagram.com/pinkberry_pl/), [@arcobaleno.beauty.bar](https://www.instagram.com/arcobaleno.beauty.bar/)).

Sensitivities to respect: avoid 🇷🇺 or "русский маникюр" branding in paid creative (use "manicure kombinowany"/"combined manicure" in PL/EN); avoid mixing UA and RU in one creative; let Meta language targeting (Russian-speaking Belarusians) deliver a Russian ad set separately if used at all; never imply the salon is "for Ukrainians only", since Poles and expats are also target clients.

## 6. Gaps and opportunities for SVORA

1. **Own the art niche in paid media.** Across 40 Wola/Bemowo listings, none markets 3D/"crazy" art; the only art-forward naming is MANIFEST in Mokotów ([Fresha](https://www.fresha.com/lp/en/bt/nails/in/pl-warsaw/ko%C5%82o)). Reels of Extra Long & Crazy Shape / Cat Claws builds are a creative asset no neighbour can copy quickly, and they justify 190+ better than "hybrid manicure" copy.
2. **Sell the Nude side with a non-discount hook.** Market norm is -10% first visit. A "free nude/French design on your first hybrid" or "free hybrid removal on first visit" (worth 60 PLN, i.e. 24% of the all-in price) beats 10% without cutting the headline price; it also neutralises PRO BEAUTY's "179 incl. removal" ([Booksy Wola](https://booksy.com/pl-pl/s/paznokcie/32248_wola)).
3. **Guarantee as standard.** Pala, Nails Design and Nailash all advertise 7-14 day free repair ([Booksy Wola](https://booksy.com/pl-pl/s/paznokcie/32248_wola)). SVORA lists none on its site ([svora.place](https://svora.place)). Add a 14-day guarantee to ad copy; it costs little at 5.0 quality.
4. **Bundle mani + pedi.** Competitors sell bundles at 306-390; SVORA's unbundled hybrid + hybrid pedicure is 425. A "Nude Duo" at ~380 would be price-competitive with Martelle's premium bundle and is a natural Ukrainian-audience offer (pedicure demand is high in diaspora listings: LiKriSo, Senchilova, Yaroslav all lead with pedicure prices, [Fresha](https://www.fresha.com/lp/en/bt/nails/in/pl-warsaw/ko%C5%82o)).
5. **English-first creative for expats.** Only a handful of bios lead with English (Nail Zone, Arcobaleno), and none of the Booksy top-20 state languages ([IG](https://www.instagram.com/nail_zone_warszawa_/), [Booksy Wola](https://booksy.com/pl-pl/s/paznokcie/32248_wola)). SVORA already has an EN site and EN-speaking staff; an English ad set targeting Wola/Śródmieście expats with Fresha (English UI) as landing is close to uncontested.
6. **Ukrainian-language ad set, DM as secondary CTA.** The 40K @warszawa.nails account proves the diaspora books through Instagram DM ([IG](https://www.instagram.com/warszawa.nails/)). Run UA creatives with Fresha deep link plus "напишіть у Direct" so clients who distrust booking forms still convert. Keep Russian to service-language disclosure, not ad copy (section 4).
7. **Close the review gap and consider a Booksy presence.** SVORA has 107-117 reviews on Fresha; neighbours have 646-2,824 on Booksy, and Booksy search is sponsored at the top ([Booksy Wola](https://booksy.com/pl-pl/s/paznokcie/32248_wola)). Either list on Booksy for discovery (accepting Boost economics) or run a post-visit review prompt in UA/PL/EN to lift Fresha and Google counts, which the ad landing page can show.
8. **Hours are parity, same-day is not.** 9-21 daily matches The Kitchen, Xnail and Arcobaleno; Pala runs to 22 ([Booksy](https://booksy.com/pl-pl/197935_the-kitchen_paznokcie_3_warszawa), [IG](https://www.instagram.com/xnail_wola/), [Booksy](https://booksy.com/pl-pl/219863_pala-nails-yuliia_paznokcie_3_warszawa)). The hook is "free slot today/tomorrow", which Fresha can surface in ad creative via live availability; no competitor advertises it.
9. **An entry tier for the price-sensitive Pole.** The Kitchen uses trainee/one-step tiers at 110-165 to catch the 140-160 shopper ([Booksy](https://booksy.com/pl-pl/197935_the-kitchen_paznokcie_3_warszawa)). SVORA's single-phase manicure (175) and CLEAN (120) can play the same role if ads name them explicitly instead of leading with 190.
10. **Cross-sell brows in the same visit.** Arcobaleno and Thanks win on "everything in one place" ([IG](https://www.instagram.com/arcobaleno.beauty.bar/), [IG](https://www.instagram.com/thanks.salon.urody/)). SVORA's brows from 70 and lamination set at 175 make a "nails + brows in one slot" creative credible and raise ticket size without discounting.
